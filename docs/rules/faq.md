# FAQ

## What is the difference between a gate and an algorithm?
A gate is an individual operation used to build a circuit. An algorithm is a goal card that scores points when its requirements are met.

## Can multiple players complete the same algorithm?
Yes, algorithms are typically available to multiple players, but once claimed, some may become unavailable depending on the variant.

## How does scoring work?
Each algorithm grants points when completed. Additional points may be awarded for efficient use of gates and high stability. **Important:** Points are recorded instantly when a Measurement succeeds, but the cards are then immediately discarded to the global Discard Pile. Players do not keep physical cards; they track points using pen and paper, a digital tracker, or custom tokens.

## What is the Card Swap (Quantum Fluctuation) action?
Exactly once per turn during your Action Phase, you may discard exactly 2 cards from your hand or Laboratory to the global Discard Pile and immediately draw 2 new cards from the main draw deck. After this draw, the main draw deck must be thoroughly reshuffled. This is a free action that helps prevent hand deadlock and adds strategic flexibility.

## Does the Card Swap count as my one action per turn?
No. The Card Swap is a free action and does not count as one of your standard Action Phase activities. You can perform it in addition to playing gates, placing algorithms, or interfering with opponents. However, you may only perform it **once per turn**.

## What happens when we run out of cards in the main draw deck?
When the main draw deck is exhausted, immediately shuffle the entire global Discard Pile to form a new draw deck and continue playing without interruption. This ensures cards remain in circulation throughout the game.

## Do I keep the cards after a successful Measurement?
No. Whether a Measurement is successful or failed, all involved cards (the Algorithm, all Gates in the sequence, and any Special cards used in reactions) are immediately sent to the global Discard Pile. You record your points using an external tracker and then lose possession of the cards. This ensures the 100-card deck circulates continuously and prevents card lockout.
