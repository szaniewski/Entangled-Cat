# Quickstart

Entangled Cat is a card game where players assemble quantum circuits, manage stability, and execute algorithms before opponents collapse the system.

1. Choose a deck of gate, algorithm, and effect cards.
2. Draw cards, play gates, and build your algorithm.
3. Use defense and attack cards to protect or disrupt.
4. Collapse your algorithm by measuring at the right moment.

## Core concepts

- Gates: build quantum operations.
- Algorithms: goals that score points.
- Stability: the durability of your circuit.
- Decoherence: the risk of losing an algorithm before completion.
