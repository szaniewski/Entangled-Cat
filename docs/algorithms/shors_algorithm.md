# Shor’s Algorithm

Points: 40
Deck Count: 1

Required Gates: H, CNOT + CR2 + CR3 + SWAP, QFT, MZ

Description: Uses modular exponentiation and QFT to factor numbers with quantum speedup.

Educational Notes:
- Shor’s Algorithm is a landmark quantum factoring protocol.
- The QFT stage is the heart of the quantum advantage.
